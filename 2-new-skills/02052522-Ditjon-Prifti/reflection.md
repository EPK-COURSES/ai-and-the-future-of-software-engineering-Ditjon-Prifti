# Reflection

Working on this research project helped me better understand how artificial intelligence is changing software engineering. Before starting the research, I already knew about tools such as ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot, but I viewed them mainly as helpful coding assistants. After researching the topic in depth, I realized that AI is becoming a major part of modern software development and is changing how software engineers work on a daily basis.

One of the things that surprised me most was how quickly AI-assisted development has been adopted by developers and companies. I learned that AI is not only used for generating code, but also for testing, debugging, documentation, and many other development activities. This showed me that software engineering is evolving faster than I previously thought.

During my research, I also realized that AI is not replacing software engineers as easily as many people believe. Instead, it is changing which skills are most valuable. While technical programming knowledge remains important, skills such as critical thinking, system design, communication, testing, verification, and adaptability are becoming increasingly valuable. If AI can generate code in seconds, engineers must be able to evaluate whether that code is correct, secure, efficient, and appropriate for the problem being solved.

Another interesting lesson was the importance of prompt engineering. I had not previously considered communication with AI systems to be a technical skill. However, I learned that the quality of AI-generated results often depends on the quality of the instructions provided by the user. This means that developers must learn how to communicate clearly not only with people, but also with AI tools.

At the same time, this research raised some concerns for me. I believe there is a risk that developers may become too dependent on AI systems and stop developing strong problem-solving skills. If engineers rely on AI without understanding the code being generated, software quality and security could suffer. Because of this, I think human judgment and verification will remain essential even as AI becomes more powerful.

Overall, this research changed my perspective on the future of software engineering. I now believe that successful software engineers will be those who can combine technical knowledge with critical thinking, creativity, communication, and continuous learning. Personally, I plan to embrace AI tools as assistants while continuing to strengthen my own understanding of software engineering fundamentals. I believe the best approach is not to compete with AI, but to learn how to use it effectively while maintaining the human skills that AI cannot easily replace.

This project helped me better understand both the opportunities and challenges that AI brings to software engineering. It also encouraged me to think more seriously about the skills I will need to develop in order to remain successful in the future technology industry.
